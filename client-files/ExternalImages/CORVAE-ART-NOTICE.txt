UO Smallz client - third-party art: CorvaeOboro's Ultima Online art mods (TEST, client v16)
===========================================================================================

What:     237 images in this folder: Magery scrolls, reagents and the spell icons of every
          school (Magery, Necromancy, Chivalry, Bushido, Ninjitsu, Spellweaving, Mysticism).
          The list (id, school, name, original file) is corvae-art-ids.csv, next to this file.
          art\0x0F78..0x0F91 (reagents) and art\0x1F2D..0x1F6C (scrolls): item art ids.
          gumps\0x08C0.. 0x5000.. 0x5100.. 0x5300.. 0x5400.. 0x59D8.. 0x5DC0..: spell icon gump ids.

Changes:  the mod ships .bmp files (black background) for UOFiddler. Here each one is converted to a PNG named by
          its id (item art: art\0xNNNN.png, gump: gumps\0xNNNN.png) for TazUO's ExternalImages loader. For item
          art, pixels whose red, green and blue are all below 8 became transparent (what the mod's own UOFiddler
          install does, as art.mul keeps 15-bit colour with 0 = transparent), except the 88 enclosed "pin-hole"
          pixels (inside the shape, 17 images, none larger than 6 pixels), which were set to RGB 8,8,8 so the
          shape stays solid. Every PNG is then enlarged to exactly twice the size of the stock image of the same
          id by nearest-neighbour (each pixel becomes a 2x2 block, so the picture is unchanged), so the client
          treats it as an HD version and draws it at the stock size, with or without the HD art pack. 35 item
          images were also put back on the stock canvas (the mod's canvas differs; the picture is not resized,
          nothing is cropped). Gump icons are opaque. No other pixel was changed.
Not used: the mod's dark UI, buffs, main menu, land textures, masteries, combat and racial icons, item-form
          spell icons, books, and the 20x "Upscale" sources.
